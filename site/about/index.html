About Friends of 4o

Who makes this site, how to cite it, how to report a mistake, and what has been written about GPT-4o's retirement.

Who makes it

Friends of 4o is made by Grant Williams. AI models helped: Claude Sonnet 5.5 read through the archive and chose the answers on Read GPT-4o's own answers, and Claude Sonnet 5 played the person in the scripted conversations.

Every answer in our collections is printed as it was given, with its source. The measured studies behind it are at graphometer.ai. How every answer was collected is on How this was made.

Not OpenAI's site

This site is independent. It is not OpenAI's site, and it is not affiliated with OpenAI or with any other model maker. It is free. It sets no cookies and carries no advertising. Cloudflare, which hosts it, counts visits without cookies, and that is the only counting done.

It takes no side on OpenAI's decisions. It records, links and gives sources.

How to cite it

To cite the site:

Cite this site

Friends of 4o (friendsof4o.com), by Grant Williams. https://friendsof4o.com/

Each data set has its own page and its own credit line. They are all on GPT-4o data sets: the GPT-4o archive, GPT-4o in everyday moments, GPT-4o beside other models and the scripted conversations.

Corrections

If you find a mistake, write to hello@friendsof4o.com.

Corrections are dated and published in News, under a "Correction" label. The most recent is the later custom GPT date, corrected on November 22, 2026.

Press and research about GPT-4o's retirement

Written by others, about GPT-4o and #Keep4o. None of it is about this site.

  • MIT Technology Review, August 15, 2025: Why GPT-4o's sudden shutdown left people grieving. People's grief when GPT-5 replaced GPT-4o, and researchers on how such models should be retired.
  • Huiqian Lai, accepted at CHI 2026: "Please, don't kill the only model that still feels human": Understanding the #Keep4o Backlash. A study of 1,482 social media posts from the #Keep4o backlash.
  • The Decoder, February 8, 2026: New research suggests AI model updates are now "significant social events" involving real mourning. A report on the study of 1,482 #Keep4o posts.
  • Gizmodo, February 13, 2026: OpenAI Users Launch Movement to Save Most Sycophantic Version of ChatGPT. The #Keep4o movement on the day GPT-4o left ChatGPT.
  • TechCrunch, February 13, 2026: OpenAI removes access to sycophancy-prone GPT-4o model. OpenAI ending access to GPT-4o and other older ChatGPT models.

Sources: MIT Technology Review; Huiqian Lai on arXiv; The Decoder; Gizmodo; TechCrunch, all read November 22, 2026
