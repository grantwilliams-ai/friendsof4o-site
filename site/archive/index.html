        About this archive

        These are answers from chatgpt-4o-latest, the GPT-4o model that ChatGPT itself ran. They were collected through OpenAI's API between December 25, 2025 and February 15, 2026. GPT-4o left ChatGPT on February 13, 2026.

        Each question was asked on its own, with nothing else in the conversation. 1,557 answers have no instruction on record. 600 were asked in February 2026 after a short instruction, and each of those says so. Many questions were asked more than once, so one question can have several different answers.

        Nothing has been edited or removed. 40 long answers stopped at a length limit set when they were collected, and each one is marked "Cut off".

        What to keep in mind

        • These are a model's words. Some facts in them are wrong, and some quotations credited to real people may be invented. Stories told in the first person are made up.
        • Some answers are about grief, illness and death. They are here so the record is complete. They are not advice. If you need support, start here.
        • The GPT-4o people knew ran inside ChatGPT, with a system prompt, memory and conversation history. These are single questions sent through the API.
        • The questions were written for these collections. No one's private conversations are here.
        • The picked answers were chosen by Claude Sonnet helpers that read the whole archive. The topic labels are loose, and an answer can sit under more than one.
