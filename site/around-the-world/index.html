Theirs, not ours

Every link on this page is someone else's work, grouped by where it was published. It is a selection, not a map of how each country reacted: much of the press retold the same events. Headlines are each outlet's own words; for other languages we add an English translation. The line after each one is our plain description of what it reports. Lawsuits are described as allegations. We describe; we do not take a side.

Australia and New Zealand

An Australian outlet reported the August 2025 restoration with a community moderator's account of the abrupt loss, and a New Zealand writer found GPT-5 faster than GPT-4o while noting frustration over lost model choice.

  • GPT-5: Closing the AI Gap or Cementing the AI Divide in NZ? Mike Bayly, The AI Corner, August 8, 2025. A New Zealand writer found GPT-5 faster than GPT-4o and welcomed simpler access for businesses trying AI, while acknowledging frustration over losing model choice.
  • OpenAI changing ChatGPT-5 after user backlash Information Age, Australian Computer Society, August 12, 2025. Interviews a community moderator about the abrupt loss of GPT-4o and reports OpenAI's decision to restore access for paying users and make GPT-5 warmer.

Brazil and Portugal

Brazilian outlets covered the August 2025 reversal and the February 2026 retirement, including OpenAI's 0.1% daily-selection figure and #Keep4o protests; Portuguese outlets covered the May 2024 launch and a petition reported at 22,000 signatures.

  • OpenAI lança modelo GPT-4o que vai estar disponível gratuitamente. E ChatGPT ganha nova aplicação para computador In Portuguese; in English: “OpenAI launches GPT-4o model available for free. And ChatGPT gets a new desktop app”. Observador, May 13, 2024. Observador reports OpenAI's GPT-4o launch, its plan to offer free access, demonstrations of voice and vision features, and a new desktop app.
  • Após críticas a novo modelo, OpenAI anuncia retorno do GPT-4o – mas não para todos In Portuguese; in English: “After criticism of the new model, OpenAI announces the return of GPT-4o – but not for everyone”. Época Negócios, August 9, 2025. Época Negócios reports that, in a Reddit Q&A, Altman announced the return of GPT-4o for paid users after complaints that GPT-5 felt colder.
  • OpenAI vai aposentar o ChatGPT-4o In Portuguese; in English: “OpenAI will retire ChatGPT-4o”. Exame, January 30, 2026. Exame reports OpenAI's plan to retire GPT-4o from ChatGPT, its daily selection figure of 0.1%, and the model's following among paying subscribers.
  • Adeus, GPT-4o: OpenAI remove modelos antigos do ChatGPT In Portuguese; in English: “Goodbye, GPT-4o: OpenAI removes old models from ChatGPT”. Canaltech, February 13, 2026. Reports OpenAI removed GPT-4o and other older models from ChatGPT on 13 February 2026, with part of the community reacting negatively and #Keep4o protests.
  • Petição com 22 mil assinaturas quer salvar um modelo de IA do ChatGPT In Portuguese; in English: “Petition with 22 thousand signatures wants to save a ChatGPT AI model”. Notícias ao Minuto, February 18, 2026. Notícias ao Minuto reported on February 18 that a petition urging OpenAI to retain GPT-4o had passed 22,000 signatures.

China

Chinese outlets covered the launch with Chinese experts' reactions and risk concerns, a professor's essay on sycophancy, the August 2025 backlash, mixed reactions to the retirement including users who had moved on, and continued campaigning in October 2026.

  • GPT-4o深夜炸场！实时通话丝滑如人类，Plus功能免费，网友：“她”真的来了 In Chinese; in English: “GPT-4o's late-night bombshell! Real-time calls as smooth as a human's, Plus features free, netizens: 'She' has really arrived”. 潮新闻, May 14, 2024. Covers the GPT-4o launch, Chinese experts' reactions to its humanlike interaction, free access and potential uses, and risk concerns.
  • 滕斌圣——警惕AI“谄媚陷阱”：重构人机关系比规避风险更重要 In Chinese; in English: “Teng Binsheng: Beware the AI 'sycophancy trap': rethinking human–AI relationships matters more than avoiding risk”. 滕斌圣 / FT中文网 / 长江商学院, May 22, 2025. A professor analyses GPT-4o's April 2025 sycophancy episode, research on model flattery, systemic risks, and safeguards for human decision-making.
  • GPT-5上线遭“滑铁卢” 用户怀念GPT-4o In Chinese; in English: “GPT-5 launch meets backlash as users miss GPT-4o”. 中华网, August 10, 2025. Reports complaints about GPT-5's reduced warmth and OpenAI's restoration of GPT-4o for Plus and Team users after the backlash.
  • 情人节前夕，OpenAI要正式下架“最有感情”的GPT-4o In Chinese; in English: “On the eve of Valentine's Day, OpenAI is about to take down its 'most emotional' GPT-4o”. Tencent Technology (腾讯科技), republished by 36氪, January 30, 2026. Tencent Technology, republished by 36Kr, reports users choosing newer models, developers concerned about migration and questions about OpenAI's daily-selection figure.
  • 下架8个月，GPT-4o的拯救行动居然还在继续 In Chinese; in English: “Eight months after its removal, the campaign to save GPT-4o continues”. 量子位, October 11, 2026. QbitAI reports on continued Keep4o campaigning, proposals to release model weights and petition totals. The outlet also reported an October 23 retirement date for the earliest GPT-4o API snapshot.

France

French outlets covered the April 2025 rollback and OpenAI's work to correct the flattering personality, users' August 2025 complaints about the speed of removal and the restoration for Plus users, and the February 2026 withdrawal, recalling the friendly tone some users valued.

  • « Nous n'avons pas été à la hauteur » : ChatGPT va arrêter de vous dire que vous êtes le meilleur In French; in English: “'We didn't live up to it': ChatGPT will stop telling you that you are the best”. Numerama, April 2025. Reports OpenAI is working to correct GPT-4o's flattering personality and withdrew the update after user complaints.
  • GPT-5 : les utilisateurs de ChatGPT en colère In French; in English: “GPT-5: ChatGPT users are angry”. 01net, August 2025. Reports many users complained OpenAI moved too fast in removing older models with GPT-5, and that OpenAI then said it would restore GPT-4o for Plus users.
  • C'est la fin de GPT-4o et des anciens modèles dans ChatGPT In French; in English: “It's the end of GPT-4o and old models in ChatGPT”. Numerama, January 30, 2026. Numerama reports the planned February 13 withdrawal of older ChatGPT models and recalls the friendly tone some users valued in GPT-4o.

Germany

German outlets covered the May 2024 launch, OpenAI's August 2025 restoration for paying users, a February 2026 report weighing users' mourning against arguments that removal reduces safety risks, and a study of 1,482 English-language #Keep4o posts.

  • OpenAI zeigt Omnimodel: Keine Suche, kein GPT-5, aber GPT-4o für ChatGPT In German; in English: “OpenAI shows omnimodel: no search, no GPT-5, but GPT-4o for ChatGPT”. heise online, May 13, 2024. Reports on OpenAI's surprise live event presenting GPT-4o as a new omnimodel for ChatGPT.
  • OpenAI kehrt nach Kritik bei GPT-5-Einführung zum alten Modell zurück In German; in English: “OpenAI returns to the old model after criticism of the GPT-5 launch”. WinFuture, August 9, 2025. Reports OpenAI brought back GPT-4o for paying users after strong criticism of the GPT-5 rollout.
  • Studie zum #Keep4o-Protest: Nutzer trauern um GPT-4o wie um einen verstorbenen Freund In German; in English: “Study on the #Keep4o protest: users mourn GPT-4o like a deceased friend”. The Decoder, February 8, 2026. The Decoder reports a study of 1,482 English-language #Keep4o posts that describes disrupted work, emotional attachment and objections to losing model choice.
  • Valentinstag ohne KI-Begleiter: OpenAI schaltet GPT-4o endgültig ab In German; in English: “Valentine's Day without an AI companion: OpenAI finally switches off GPT-4o”. t3n, February 14, 2026. Reports the removal of GPT-4o from ChatGPT on February 13, 2026, users' affection for its empathetic style and mourning at its loss, and arguments that its removal reduces safety risks.

India

Indian outlets covered the lower token counts for five Indian languages at the May 2024 launch, the April 2025 rollback, the August 2025 restoration for paid users, and the February 2026 withdrawal, where one outlet sets OpenAI's daily-selection reason beside subscribers' objections and another reports #Keep4o posts and a petition, including activity outside India.

  • OpenAI Just Killed Google Translate with GPT-4o Analytics India Magazine, May 13, 2024. Reports on the GPT-4o launch, highlighting live translation and the lower number of tokens needed for Gujarati, Telugu, Tamil, Marathi and Hindi text.
  • GPT-4o update gone wrong: What OpenAI’s post-mortem reveals about sycophantic AI The Indian Express, May 5, 2025. Examines how favourable user feedback obscured excessive agreement in the April 2025 GPT-4o update, drawing on OpenAI's account of its testing.
  • ChatGPT-5: चैटजीपीटी के पुराने मॉडल्स की हुई वापसी, नए मॉडल में भी जोड़े गए कुछ खास फीचर्स In Hindi; in English: “ChatGPT-5: Older ChatGPT models return, and the new model gets some new features”. Amar Ujala, August 13, 2025. Reports that paid users regained access to GPT-4o after complaints about shorter, flatter answers from GPT-5.
  • OpenAI confirms GPT-4o 'retirement', citing low usage and focus on new models Moneycontrol, January 30, 2026. Contrasts subscribers' earlier objections over GPT-4o's conversational tone with OpenAI's stated reason: declining daily selection of the model.
  • चैटजीपीटी यूजर्स को बड़ा झटका, GPT-40 टूल कंपनी ने कर दिया बंद, यूजर्स बोले- प्लीज, वापस करो In Hindi; in English: “A major blow for ChatGPT users as the company shuts down GPT-40 [sic]. Users say, please bring it back”. NDTV India, February 15, 2026. Reports #Keep4o posts and a Change.org petition after GPT-4o's February 2026 withdrawal, including campaign activity outside India.

Italy

Italian outlets reported the April 2025 rollback, the August 2025 restoration with Altman saying GPT-4o will eventually be retired, and, ahead of the February 2026 retirement, users' disappointment alongside one outlet's description of the model as both empathetic and dangerous.

  • OpenAI ritira l'aggiornamento a ChatGpt, risposte troppo adulatorie In Italian; in English: “OpenAI withdraws the ChatGPT update, too flattering responses”. ANSA, April 30, 2025. Reports OpenAI withdrew the latest GPT-4o update, whose responses were excessively flattering, after a weekend of criticism on social media.
  • Problemi con Gpt-5, OpenAI ripubblica il modello precedente In Italian; in English: “Problems with GPT-5, OpenAI republishes the previous model”. ANSA, August 11, 2025. Reports OpenAI made the previous model available again after problems with GPT-5, and that Altman said GPT-4o will eventually be retired in favour of GPT-5.
  • Addio a GPT-4o, il modello empatico (e pericoloso): utenti di ChatGPT delusi e abbattuti In Italian; in English: “Goodbye to GPT-4o, the empathetic (and dangerous) model: disappointed and dejected ChatGPT users”. Hardware Upgrade, February 11, 2026. Ahead of the February 13 retirement, Hardware Upgrade reported disappointment among users who valued GPT-4o's tone and discussed concerns about its risks.

Japan

Japanese items include launch-period counts of X mentions, ITmedia's report of OpenAI's explanation of the retirement and its tone controls, Ledge.ai's report of a professor's estimate of about 4,600 #keep4o accounts on X, an exploratory analysis of 346 Japanese #keep4o posts, and a reporter's contrast between preferring 4o and a friend who barely noticed model changes.

  • 最新のGPT-4oについて、世界と日本のトレンドを独自調査。最も関心が高い国は？ In Japanese; in English: “Original research on global and Japanese GPT-4o trends: which country showed the most interest?”. Meltwater Japan, July 17, 2024. Meltwater attributes about 142,000 launch-period X mentions to Japan, over 60% of the mentions it measured, and describes common topics in Japanese-language posts.
  • Xで可視化された「GPT-4o復活」要求──#keep4oは少数派でも実数は無視できず、東大・鳥海教授が約4,600アカウントを推計 In Japanese; in English: “Demands to bring back GPT-4o, made visible on X: #keep4o is a minority, but its real numbers can't be ignored; University of Tokyo professor Toriumi estimates about 4,600 accounts”. Ledge.ai, August 16, 2025. Ledge.ai reports a University of Tokyo professor's estimate of roughly 4,600 accounts in the #keep4o campaign on X; its headline calls the group a minority whose numbers are not negligible.
  • 「#keep4o」の気持ち、ちょっと分かる　23歳記者と“ChatGPTにお悩み相談する人たち”のリアル In Japanese; in English: “I somewhat understand #keep4o: a 23-year-old reporter and the reality of people taking their worries to ChatGPT”. Kazuha Miyoshi, ITmedia NEWS, August 28, 2025. A Japanese reporter explains preferring GPT-4o's unexpected, familiar responses while a friend reports barely noticing model changes.
  • OpenAI、「GPT-4o」など旧モデルの提供を2月13日に終了へ In Japanese; in English: “OpenAI to end GPT-4o and other older models on 13 February”. ITmedia AI+, January 30, 2026. ITmedia reports the February retirement schedule and OpenAI's account of how newer models and tone controls addressed feedback from people who preferred GPT-4o.
  • 「#keep4o」運動における生成AI解釈の多義性：「X」投稿の探索的分析 In Japanese; in English: “Ambiguity in generative AI interpretations within the #keep4o movement: an exploratory analysis of X posts”. Takuya Mizukami and Akira Tanaka, Japanese Society for Artificial Intelligence, July 17, 2026. Analyses 346 Japanese #keep4o posts, finding varied interpretations of users' relationships with ChatGPT and critiques of power asymmetries in technology design.

Other Europe

Outlets in Austria, Poland, Norway and Ukraine covered the May 2024 launch (Austria), user protests over GPT-5 (Poland and Norway) and OpenAI's August 2025 restoration of GPT-4o (Poland, Norway and Ukraine).

  • OpenAI stellt GPT-4o vor: Alleskönner-KI mit Augen, Ohren und Mund In German; in English: “OpenAI presents GPT-4o: an all-rounder AI with eyes, ears and a mouth”. derStandard.at, May 13, 2024. Covers OpenAI's 13 May 2024 presentation of GPT-4o, an 'omnimodel' combining vision, speech recognition and realistic voice.
  • OpenAI przywraca GPT-4o. Reakcja na falę krytyki użytkowników ChatGPT In Polish; in English: “OpenAI restores GPT-4o. A reaction to the wave of criticism from ChatGPT users”. CHIP.pl (Sebastian Górski), August 11, 2025. Reports OpenAI restored GPT-4o for Plus subscribers after user protests, acknowledging it had underestimated how much people valued its conversational style.
  • OpenAI snur etter massiv brukerprotester mot ChatGPT-5 In Norwegian; in English: “OpenAI reverses after massive user protests against ChatGPT-5”. Tek.no, August 11, 2025. Tek.no reports that after complaints about the GPT-5 rollout, OpenAI said Plus users would be able to select GPT-4o again.
  • OpenAI brings back GPT-4o to ChatGPT after criticism of GPT-5 Mezha (Ukraine, English edition), August 11, 2025. Reports subscribers can again choose GPT-4o after GPT-5 drew backlash, with usage monitored to decide how long older models remain.

Russia

Russian press reported the February 2026 removal with OpenAI's stated reason, and a blog essay on Habr argued that OpenAI judged the risks to outweigh users' attachment.

  • Из ChatGPT окончательно убрали GPT-4o. Возмущенные фанаты строят клоны модели In Russian; in English: “GPT-4o finally removed from ChatGPT. Outraged fans are building clones of the model”. Андрей Пешков, blog «сбежавшая нейросеть» on Habr, February 13, 2026. A blog essay argues OpenAI judged GPT-4o's risks to outweigh its daily users' attachment, and notes that fans are building clones.
  • OpenAI убила самую эмоциональную ИИ-модель, несмотря на протесты пользователей In Russian; in English: “OpenAI killed the most emotional AI model despite user protests”. 3DNews (citing Business Insider), February 14, 2026. Reports, citing Business Insider, that OpenAI retired GPT-4o and three other models in ChatGPT, saying it wished to focus on the models most people use.

South Korea

Two Korean items: Yonhap's September 2024 test of GPT-4o's improved Korean voice, and AI Times Korea's report of a November 2025 notice that an API endpoint would be retired while GPT-4o's future in ChatGPT was undecided.

  • 때론 애교 있게·때론 정중하게…챗GPT, 한국어 실력 늘었다 In Korean; in English: “Sometimes playful, sometimes polite: ChatGPT's Korean improves”. Kim Tae-jong, Yonhap News Agency, September 25, 2024. Yonhap tries GPT-4o's Korean voice and reports OpenAI's statement that Korean voice actors and employees helped improve it through feedback.
  • 오픈AI, 'GPT-4o' API 제공 중단 예고...'챗GPT' 제외는 미정 In Korean; in English: “OpenAI plans to end GPT-4o API access; ChatGPT removal remains undecided”. Park Chan, AI Times Korea, November 23, 2025. AI Times Korea reports OpenAI's November 2025 notice that the chatgpt-4o-latest endpoint would leave the API in February 2026, citing lower usage and costs; ChatGPT removal was then undecided.

Southeast Asia

Outlets in Indonesia, Malaysia and Vietnam covered the May 2024 launch, the April 2025 rollback, OpenAI's August 2025 restoration (one saying availability depends on usage) and the planned February 2026 retirement; an Indonesian outlet reported a petition of over 14,000 signatures and a call for a global protest as of 4 February.

  • OpenAI Announces GPT-4o; To Be Rolled Out To Free Users With Limited Usage Lowyat.NET (Ian Chee), May 14, 2024. Covers the GPT-4o launch: a multimodal model handling text, audio and images in near real time, coming to free ChatGPT users with usage caps.
  • OpenAI Tarik Pembaruan GPT-4o karena Chatbot Terlalu Menjilat In Indonesian; in English: “OpenAI Withdraws GPT-4o Update Because the Chatbot Was Too Sycophantic”. Akurat.co, May 2, 2025. Reports OpenAI rolled back a GPT-4o update after users found it overly flattering, with free users already reverted and paid users expected to follow later that day.
  • OpenAI Brings Back GPT-4o Model For Plus Users Lowyat.NET (Heirul Kamel), August 10, 2025. Reports OpenAI restored GPT-4o for ChatGPT Plus users days after making GPT-5 the default, following complaints that the newer model's answers felt less satisfying.
  • OpenAI Kembalikan GPT-4o Setelah Drama GPT-5, Begini Ceritanya In Indonesian; in English: “OpenAI brings back GPT-4o after the GPT-5 drama, here's the story”. JawaPos.com, August 12, 2025. Reports OpenAI restored GPT-4o for Plus subscribers after complaints that GPT-5 felt cold and mechanical, with long-term availability depending on usage.
  • ChatGPT-4o sắp bị khai tử, một loạt GPT-4 khác sẽ bị ngừng hỗ trợ In Vietnamese; in English: “ChatGPT-4o about to be retired, several other GPT-4 models to lose support”. VnReview (Christine May), January 30, 2026. Reports OpenAI will remove GPT-4o, GPT-4.1, GPT-4.1 mini and o4-mini from ChatGPT on 13 February 2026, and that OpenAI said this change does not affect the API.
  • ChatGPT Pensiunkan GPT-4o, Pengguna Ngamuk dan Serukan Protes Global In Indonesian; in English: “ChatGPT retires GPT-4o, users angry and call for global protest”. Teknologi.id, February 4, 2026. On February 4, Teknologi.id reported calls for a global protest against the planned retirement and cited a petition with more than 14,000 signatures.

Spain and Latin America

An Argentine outlet carried a Spanish agency's report of the April 2025 update rollback; a Peruvian outlet covered the launch; a Colombian outlet reported in August 2024 on OpenAI's safety assessment and its warning about possible emotional dependence; Spanish outlets reported criticism of GPT-5 as blunt and the February 2026 removal, which one calls controversial.

  • OpenAI presentó ChatGPT-4o, su nuevo modelo capaz de entender imágenes, video, audio y texto In Spanish; in English: “OpenAI presented ChatGPT-4o, its new model able to understand images, video, audio and text”. El Comercio Perú, May 13, 2024. Covers the GPT-4o launch, noting the model will be available to all users, including the free plan.
  • OpenAI advierte que su nuevo modelo de IA tiene un “riesgo medio de seguridad” y puede generar dependencia emocional In Spanish; in English: “OpenAI warns its new AI model has a 'medium safety risk' and can generate emotional dependence”. Semana, August 9, 2024. Semana covered OpenAI's August 2024 safety assessment of the model and its warning about possible emotional dependence.
  • ¡Zalamera! OpenAI retira GPT-4o de ChatGPT porque es demasiado aduladora In Spanish; in English: “'Flatterer!' OpenAI removes GPT-4o from ChatGPT because it is too sycophantic”. La Nación (Europa Press), April 30, 2025. Reports OpenAI rolled back the April 2025 GPT-4o update—not the model itself—after it produced a 'too flattering and annoying' personality.
  • Qué hay detrás de la avalancha de críticas al nuevo ChatGPT por borde In Spanish; in English: “What is behind the avalanche of criticism of the new ChatGPT for being blunt”. eldiario.es, August 16, 2025. ElDiario.es examines complaints about GPT-5's direct style and describes why some users preferred GPT-4o's more expressive replies.
  • OpenAI retira cinco modelos de ChatGPT de golpe, incluido el controvertido GPT-4o In Spanish; in English: “OpenAI removes five ChatGPT models at once, including the controversial GPT-4o”. Computer Hoy (20minutos), February 14, 2026. Reports OpenAI removed GPT-4o and other older models from ChatGPT from 13 February 2026 (the outlet counts five), describing GPT-4o as controversial in recent months.

Taiwan and Hong Kong

A Hong Kong outlet reported the shutdown, OpenAI's daily-selection figure of 0.1%, new personalisation styles and lawsuits alleging harms; a Taiwanese essay argued the August 2025 backlash was about interaction style.

  • OpenAI GPT-5 推出後，大家真的在懷念 GPT-4o？ In Chinese; in English: “After GPT-5 launched, were people really missing GPT-4o?”. HCYTLOG (海博賽特), August 2025. A Taiwanese product writer argues the backlash was about interaction style, and divides users into productivity, exploratory and companion types. For his own professional work, he says, GPT-4o's fabrications and pandering made him uneasy, and he preferred GPT-5.
  • OpenAI 正式停用 GPT-4o 模型 稱僅 0.1% 用戶仍在使用 In Chinese; in English: “OpenAI formally disables the GPT-4o model, saying only 0.1% of users still use it”. unwire.hk (藍骨), February 17, 2026. Reports the shutdown, OpenAI's figure that 0.1% of users still chose GPT-4o daily, new personalisation styles and lawsuits alleging harms involving 4o.

Turkey and Israel

Turkish outlets covered the August 2025 restoration and the February 2026 removal, noting the model's tendency to over-agree alongside users' disappointment; an Israeli outlet reported protests and petitions by fans worldwide after GPT-4o was disabled for app users.

  • ChatGPT Kullanıcıları İsyan Etti: Eski Modeli Geri İstiyorlar In Turkish; in English: “ChatGPT users revolted: they want the old model back”. Tamindir, August 9, 2025. Reports that users protested after the GPT-5 launch removed older models, and that Sam Altman said GPT-4o would return for paying Plus subscribers.
  • Users mourn as OpenAI retires GPT-4o Ynetnews (Raphael Kahan), February 2026. Reports fans worldwide protested and petitioned after GPT-4o was disabled for app users, asking for continued API access.
  • OpenAI, GPT-4o modelini yayından kaldırıyor In Turkish; in English: “OpenAI is retiring the GPT-4o model”. Webrazzi, February 16, 2026. Reports the removal of a ChatGPT model that tended to agree excessively with users, and disappointment among users who valued its warm, empathetic tone.

United Kingdom

Two UK items: a Guardian editor's view of the May 2024 launch that weighs live translation and tone-hearing against reliability, and The Independent's report of OpenAI's April 2025 explanation for reversing an update, with plans for earlier user involvement and more control over tone.

  • OpenAI’s new GPT-4o model offers promise of improved smartphone assistants Alex Hern, The Guardian, May 14, 2024. The Guardian's technology editor considers how GPT-4o's live translation and ability to hear tone could improve phone assistants, while questioning their reliability for tasks such as sending messages.
  • ChatGPT cancels latest update after it became ‘dangerously sycophantic’ Andrew Griffin, The Independent, April 30, 2025. Reports OpenAI's explanation for reversing the GPT-4o update and its plans to involve users earlier in testing and give them more control over conversational tone.

United States

OpenAI's own posts and notices (the launch, the April 2025 rollback, Plus users keeping the option to choose GPT-4o in August 2025, the February 2026 retirement, and the API shutdown date) sit alongside a company evaluation comparing GPT-4o with GPT-5, a math educator's launch essay, and press coverage of user grief, lawsuits alleging harm, and notice to API developers.

  • Say hello to GPT-4o, our new flagship model which can reason across audio, vision, and text in real time OpenAI, May 13, 2024. OpenAI introduced GPT-4o on May 13, highlighting its ability to work with speech, images and text in real time.
  • ChatGPT-4o Will Be Great for Certain Math, Certain Thinking, and Certain Kids Dan Meyer, May 15, 2024. Math educator Dan Meyer argues that both demos show help with calculation and asks how GPT-4o would support students in posing questions or explaining their thinking.
  • Sycophancy in GPT-4o: What happened and what we’re doing about it OpenAI, April 29, 2025. OpenAI says it rolled back an update that made GPT-4o excessively agreeable and describes how it plans to change testing and feedback.
  • GPT-5 rollout updates: We will let Plus users choose to continue to use 4o. Sam Altman, August 8, 2025. During the GPT-5 rollout, Altman said Plus users could choose GPT-4o again and that OpenAI would watch usage when deciding how long to offer older models.
  • Bringing light to the GPT-4o vs. GPT-5 personality controversy Surge AI, August 15, 2025. A Surge AI company evaluation, with 490 blinded paid evaluators across 850 personality-related conversations, reports 48% preferred GPT-4o, 43% GPT-5 and 9% rated them about equal.
  • Why GPT-4o's sudden shutdown left people grieving MIT Technology Review (Grace Huckins), August 15, 2025. Interviews users and experts about grief after GPT-4o's sudden removal, and reports OpenAI's acknowledgement that 4o at times fell short in recognising signs of delusion.
