Ask GPT-4o a question

GPT-4o's last two versions leave OpenAI's API on January 14, 2027. Until then we can still ask it questions. Send yours and we will ask it for you.

Your question for GPT-4o

Please don't include private details. Your question and GPT-4o's answer may be published on this site, without your name.

Grant Williams reads these. We keep your question, your email address if you gave one, and the time it was sent. Nothing else is recorded. To have yours deleted, write to hello@friendsof4o.com.

What to know first

  • We ask the GPT-4o that is still on OpenAI's API (the November 2024 version). It is GPT-4o, but not the exact version ChatGPT used.
  • It will not know you. It has no memory of your conversations and none of your instructions.
  • We ask each question twice: once plainly, and once with the February instruction, which brings its answers closer to how ChatGPT's own GPT-4o spoke.
  • We may not be able to ask every question. Send yours by January 7, 2027.
