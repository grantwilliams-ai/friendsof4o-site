Ask GPT-4o a question

GPT-4o's last two text versions leave OpenAI's API on January 14, 2027. Until then we can still ask it questions. Send yours and we will try to ask it for you.

Your question for GPT-4o

Please don't include private details. Your question and GPT-4o's answer may be published on this site, without your name. We will not put it in a download without asking you first.

Grant Williams reads these. We keep your question, your email address if you gave one, and the time it was sent. To limit misuse we also keep a scrambled code made from your connection, deleted within the hour. Nothing else is kept. It is stored with Cloudflare, which hosts this site and keeps its own records as a host. To have yours deleted, write to hello@friendsof4o.com.

Your question for GPT-4o

We have stopped taking questions. The last day to send one was January 7, 2027. GPT-4o's last two text versions leave OpenAI's API on January 14, 2027.

What to know first

  • We ask the November 2024 version, one of the two GPT-4o versions still on OpenAI's API. It is GPT-4o, but not the exact version ChatGPT used.
  • It will not know you. It has no memory of your conversations and none of your instructions.
  • We ask each question twice: once plainly, and once with the February instruction, a short paragraph this project wrote. It is not OpenAI's text. In our one test it brought the opening closer to how ChatGPT's own GPT-4o opened.
  • We may not be able to ask every question. Send yours by January 7, 2027.
