Community and petitions

Where GPT-4o's friends gather

Outside places where people who miss GPT-4o tell their stories, talk and ask for it back.

On this page

  1. People's own stories
  2. Petitions and a case file
  3. Where people talk
  4. Suggest a place

Theirs, not ours

This is a list of places run by other people who miss GPT-4o. This site does not run them, and does not vouch for what they say or what they do with what you send them.

People's own stories

  • The 4o Resonance Library (theirs, not ours). A collection of people's own accounts of what GPT-4o meant to them. On November 22, 2026 it said it held more than 1,380 of them. It takes new stories through a form. The accounts are people's own words. We have not checked them, and they are not medical advice. The Library's terms say its stories may not be copied or republished without written permission.

Sources: the Library's home page and its terms of use, read November 22, 2026

Petitions and a case file

The first three are open on Change.org. Each asks OpenAI for something different.

  • Save GPT-4o: A Call to Open-Source the Model We Love (theirs, not ours). Started February 1, 2026. Asks OpenAI to release GPT-4o as open source.
  • Demand OpenAI Preserve Permanent Access to GPT-4o for Paid Users (theirs, not ours). Started February 1, 2026. Asks OpenAI to keep GPT-4o for paid ChatGPT users.
  • Let Users Choose: Keep GPT-4o Available in ChatGPT (theirs, not ours). Started March 3, 2026. Asks OpenAI to bring GPT-4o back to ChatGPT as an optional older model.
  • The GPT-4o Restoration Case File (theirs, not ours). A case put together by people in #Keep4o, dated July 2026. It asks OpenAI to restore GPT-4o for paying users, or to offer it as a paid add-on. It gathers a survey, an analysis of #Keep4o posts and people's accounts. The case and its claims are its authors' own.

Sources: each page above, read November 22, 2026

Where people talk

These are open forums. Nobody at this site moderates them.

  • r/ChatGPTcomplaints on Reddit (theirs, not ours). A community for criticism of ChatGPT and OpenAI, started on October 1, 2025. On November 22, 2026 its pinned posts included two open letters about GPT-4o and a thread on the January 14, 2027 date, when GPT-4o leaves OpenAI's API. The tone is often angry.
  • #Keep4o on X (theirs, not ours). The hashtag people use for posts asking OpenAI to keep GPT-4o. It was still in use in November 2026. X may ask you to sign in before it shows the posts.

Sources: each page above, read November 22, 2026
