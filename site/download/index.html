What changed since version 1

Version 1.3, published November 26, 2026. Version 1 was published on November 21, 2026. Versions 1.1 and 1.2 followed on November 22, and version 1.3 on November 26. The old download addresses lead to the current files.

  • The answers are the same. Not one character of any answer changed in version 1.1, 1.2 or 1.3.
  • Version 1.3 brings the rights notice inside each download into line with this page: you are welcome to use the answers too. Nothing else changed.
  • Version 1.2 corrects words in the notes and widens the rights notice: our questions, scripted lines and instructions may be reused with credit. The answer files are the same as in version 1.1.
  • Scripted conversations: a new label, same_lines_id, and a table, pairing.csv, show which conversations sent GPT-4o exactly the same person lines. The old script_id is kept as it was. It gave the one label E1 to 21 different conversations.
  • Every download now has a guide to each file's fields (FIELDS.txt), a SHA-256 checksum for each file (SHA256SUMS.txt), and a line to cite it (CITATION.txt).
  • The comparison: a list of answers that were asked again and attempts that were not kept (attempts_not_kept.csv), and a field with the sampling settings each model actually ran with.
  • The archive: each answer now carries its recorded output token count, and the 40 answers that reached a length limit say where they stopped.
  • Corrected words in the READMEs: how many times each everyday moment was run, which replies are marked garbled, the length limits, the names in the scripted conversations, and one rights notice for every download.

Using any of it

Friends of 4o offers its own material under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0): our questions, the messages and lines we scripted, our instructions (system prompts), our notes, and the selection and arrangement of each collection. You may share and adapt these if you credit "Friends of 4o (friendsof4o.com), by Grant Williams".

You are welcome to use the answers too: read them, quote them, study them and build on them. We claim no rights in them and ask nothing for them. We can't offer them under a license, because a license only covers what is ours to give, and text a model wrote may not belong to anyone. The same goes for the person's lines that Claude Sonnet 5 wrote in the scripted conversations. The model maker's terms may still apply, so check them, especially if your use is commercial or large.

No one's private conversations are in these files. The questions were written for the collections.

If you use the data for something, or you need it in another form, write to hello@friendsof4o.com.
