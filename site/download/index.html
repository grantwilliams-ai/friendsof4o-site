Using any of it

Every set is free to copy, share and adapt under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0. Please credit "Friends of 4o (friendsof4o.com), by Grant Williams". The answers themselves are model output, and each model's maker has its own terms.

No one's private conversations are in these files. The questions were written for the collections.

If you use the data for something, or you need it in another form, write to hello@friendsof4o.com.
