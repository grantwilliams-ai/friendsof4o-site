Is GPT-4o coming back?

Is GPT-4o coming back?

OpenAI has not said that it will. Here is what OpenAI has said, where GPT-4o still runs, and what people are asking for.

On this page

  1. It came back once
  2. What OpenAI said when it retired GPT-4o
  3. Where GPT-4o still runs
  4. #Keep4o: what people are asking for
  5. What you can do now
  6. If anything changes

The short answer

Not that anyone has announced. GPT-4o left ChatGPT on February 13, 2026. OpenAI's notice and its Help Center article say nothing about a return. Two versions of GPT-4o still run on OpenAI's API, for developers, until January 14, 2027.

Sources: OpenAI: Retiring GPT-4o and older models; OpenAI Help Center: retiring GPT-4o and other ChatGPT models; OpenAI's deprecations page, all read November 22, 2026

Can I still use GPT-4o?

In ChatGPT
No. GPT-4o left ChatGPT on February 13, 2026. Source: OpenAI's notice Is GPT-4o coming back?
On OpenAI's API, for developers
The two dated versions until January 14, 2027, and GPT-4o's voice models until January 20, 2027. Source: OpenAI's deprecations page
On Microsoft Azure, for its customers
Microsoft lists April 14, 2027 for the same two versions. Source: Microsoft's retirement page, read November 19, 2026
Open models
Some open models are said by their makers to sound like GPT-4o. They are different models. We asked each of them the same questions as GPT-4o, on our own computer. See the open models and read their answers
Paid services
AI Revival and Get4oBack are paid chat apps that say they offer GPT-4o; we haven't tested either, and uploading a ChatGPT export gives them your conversations. Get4oBack's page lists a May 2024 version of GPT-4o. OpenAI's API stopped serving that version on October 23, 2026, and Microsoft lists January 14, 2027 for it on Azure. Sources: OpenAI's deprecations page, Microsoft's retirement page, read November 22, 2026.

It came back once

On August 7, 2025, GPT-5 replaced GPT-4o in ChatGPT. The next day, OpenAI's chief executive said Plus users could choose to keep using GPT-4o, and by August 11 they could turn it back on in settings. On August 12, GPT-4o was back in the model picker for all paid users by default.

OpenAI later wrote that it brought GPT-4o back after hearing from some Plus and Pro users. They needed more time to move their work, and they preferred its "conversational style and warmth".

That return is why people still ask the question. It lasted six months.

Sources: ChatGPT release notes; OpenAI: Retiring GPT-4o and older models; MacRumors, August 8, 2025; The Register, August 11, 2025, read November 22, 2026

What OpenAI said when it retired GPT-4o

OpenAI announced the retirement on January 29, 2026. It said:

  • GPT-4o would leave ChatGPT on February 13, 2026.
  • Only 0.1 percent of users still chose GPT-4o each day.
  • What people had said about GPT-4o shaped the models after it, with changes to personality and new settings for warmth and enthusiasm.
  • Nothing was changing on the API at that time.

The notice does not say GPT-4o will return, and it does not say it will not. Business, Enterprise and Edu workspaces kept GPT-4o inside custom GPTs until April 3, 2026. OpenAI's Help Center says that after that date GPT-4o is fully retired on every ChatGPT plan.

Sources: OpenAI: Retiring GPT-4o and older models; OpenAI Help Center: retiring GPT-4o and other ChatGPT models, read November 22, 2026

Where GPT-4o still runs

Not in ChatGPT. These are for developers and companies, and each has an end date.

  • OpenAI's API: the August 2024 and November 2024 versions run until January 14, 2027. OpenAI names GPT-6 Luna as the replacement.
  • GPT-4o's voice models on the API: until January 20, 2027.
  • Microsoft Azure, for its customers: Microsoft lists April 14, 2027 for the same two versions.

The version ChatGPT itself used left the API on February 17, 2026. No date after these has been announced by either company.

If you do not use an API, you can still send GPT-4o a question and we will try to ask it for you before January 14.

Sources: OpenAI's deprecations page, read November 22, 2026; Microsoft's retirement page, read November 19, 2026. Every date is on the GPT-4o timeline.

#Keep4o: what people are asking for

#Keep4o is the name people gave to the push to keep GPT-4o, and then to bring it back. It began when GPT-5 replaced GPT-4o in August 2025, and it grew again in the weeks after OpenAI announced the retirement.

  • In the news. Gizmodo reported on February 13, 2026 that the movement had filled the replies to OpenAI's account on X and spread on Reddit.
  • In research. A study by Huiqian Lai, accepted at the CHI 2026 conference, read 1,482 social media posts from the #Keep4o backlash. It says the movement began when OpenAI replaced GPT-4o with GPT-5, and that people were losing two things: a tool their work depended on, and a relationship.
  • A petition to open the model. "Save GPT-4o: A Call to Open-Source the Model We Love" was started on Change.org on February 1, 2026. It asks OpenAI to release GPT-4o for research use and to keep its API versions running. It showed 476 verified signatures when we read it on November 22, 2026. Two more petitions are listed on Where GPT-4o's friends gather.

We know of no announcement from OpenAI that it will release GPT-4o or return it to ChatGPT.

We link these so that you can find them. What each one says is its own.

Sources: Gizmodo, February 13, 2026; Huiqian Lai, "Understanding the #Keep4o Backlash"; Change.org petition, all read November 22, 2026

What you can do now

  • Keep what is yours. Save your ChatGPT chats and custom GPTs. An export keeps every word GPT-4o wrote with you.
  • Read it again. GPT-4o's own answers and the GPT-4o archive are here to read and to download.
  • Find the closest model. Read other models' answers beside GPT-4o's, or see what models are out there.
  • Bring its style with you. A short instruction brought GPT-4o's way of opening back in our own test.
  • If this is hard. Support and resources for anyone grieving GPT-4o.

If anything changes

If OpenAI announces anything about GPT-4o, it will be in News with its source, and this page will change the same day. You can also join the mailing list.
