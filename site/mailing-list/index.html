Mailing list and requests

Get an email when something new is added, or tell us what you need.

Get an email when something new is added

Your address goes to Kit, the service that sends our emails, and is used only for this list. You will get one email asking you to confirm. Every email has an unsubscribe link.

Tell us what you need

Is there something about GPT-4o you are looking for and cannot find? A guide, a comparison, a model we should test? Tell us and we will try to add it.

Please don't paste private conversations.

Grant Williams reads these. We keep your message, your email address if you gave one, and the time it was sent. To limit misuse we also keep a scrambled code made from your connection. It is removed the next time anyone uses a form, the vote or the results page after its hour ends. We keep nothing else from this form. What we keep is stored with Cloudflare, which hosts this site and keeps its own records as a host. To have yours deleted, write to hello@friendsof4o.com.
