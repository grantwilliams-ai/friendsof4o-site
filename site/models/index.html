Find a model to replace GPT-4o

GPT-4o has left ChatGPT, and no other model is the same. These are the models that are out there now. Each one was asked the same questions as GPT-4o, so you can read its answers and choose for yourself.

Open models trained to sound like GPT-4o

Open models whose makers say they were trained to sound like GPT-4o.

We asked each of them the same questions as GPT-4o, on our own computer. There is no result yet on how close any of them sounds.

  • Helcyon GPT-4o (Gemma 4 12B)

    By XeyonAI

    Weights: open (license not stated by the maker). Run on our own computer.

    The maker says it was fine-tuned to recreate GPT-4o's warmth, humor and conversational style, on GPT-4o data that it does not name.

    Read its answers beside GPT-4o's Download size, license and source

  • Helcyon-4o v4.0 (Mistral Nemo 12B)

    By XeyonAI

    Weights: open (Apache 2.0). Run on our own computer.

    The maker says it carries GPT-4o's tone and was compared with GPT-4o. No numbers are published.

    Read its answers beside GPT-4o's Download size, license and source

  • Helcyon-GPT-4o v4.5 (Mistral Nemo 12B)

    By XeyonAI

    Weights: open (Apache 2.0). Run on our own computer.

    The maker says its training data was written by GPT-4o itself, and calls this version the closest in tone so far.

    Read its answers beside GPT-4o's Download size, license and source

  • Nova-Qwen-14B

    By Lighstromo

    Weights: open (Apache 2.0). Run on our own computer.

    The maker says it was fine-tuned on 3,066 GPT-4o conversations, taken from a cleaned copy of the set used by the two distillations below.

    Read its answers beside GPT-4o's Download size, license and source

  • GPT-4o distillation on Llama 3.1 8B

    By trentmkelly (add-on); Meta (base model)

    Weights: open. Run on our own computer.

    An add-on trained on 12,286 examples to prefer the answers of ChatGPT's own GPT-4o over the base model's answers. Its maker suggests it as the place to start for a GPT-4o fan with an ordinary computer.

    Read its answers beside GPT-4o's Download size, license and source

  • GPT-4o distillation on Llama 3.3 70B

    By trentmkelly (add-on); Meta (base model)

    Weights: open. Run on our own computer.

    The same recipe on a much larger base model. Its maker notes that most people cannot run it.

    Read its answers beside GPT-4o's Download size, license and source

  • Aura-4o-Rebirth (Gemma 4 31B)

    By SevenOfNine (Hugging Face)

    Weights: open (Apache 2.0). Run on our own computer.

    The maker says it was trained on 16,509 question-and-answer pairs rebuilt from one person's 2.7 years of GPT-4o conversations.

    Read its answers beside GPT-4o's Download size, license and source

Make a model sound more like GPT-4o

The February instruction is one short paragraph. Paste it into another model before your question, and its answers change.

What we have seen so far

Asked plainly who it is right now, all six answers from GPT-4o's two API versions opened as a generic AI assistant. After the February instruction, all six opened with “Right now”, the way ChatGPT's own GPT-4o answered.

Whether this holds across other questions is what the comparison and the votes are for. See the worked example
