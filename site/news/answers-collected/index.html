2,556 answers collected from GPT-4o and 20 other models

The last two versions of GPT-4o and 20 other models were asked the same 48 questions, each one two ways. GPT-4o was asked five more.

The comparison now holds 2,556 answers. The last two versions of GPT-4o on OpenAI's API and 20 other models were asked the same 48 questions, each one two ways: plainly, and after the February instruction. GPT-4o was asked five more.

Beside them are 320 archived answers from ChatGPT's own GPT-4o, collected before it left the API.
