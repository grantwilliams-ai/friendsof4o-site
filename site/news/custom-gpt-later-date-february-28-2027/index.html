Correction: the later custom GPT date is February 28, 2027

Until November 22, 2026, the Save page and the Timeline gave February 11, 2027 as the later date for custom GPTs. OpenAI's help page gives February 28, 2027. December 11, 2026 for most accounts has not changed.

Until November 22, 2026, the Save page and the Timeline on this site said that FedRAMP workspaces and accounts with an approved deferral had until February 11, 2027 to keep their custom GPTs. That date was wrong.

OpenAI's help page gives February 28, 2027 as the retirement date for FedRAMP workspaces and for Enterprise workspaces with a qualifying, approved deferral. It adds that the later date does not apply to every workspace.

Both pages now say February 28, 2027.

What has not changed

Custom GPTs retire in ChatGPT on December 11, 2026, for most accounts. The steps are on Save a custom GPT before December 11.

Source: OpenAI Help Center: custom GPT retirement, read November 22, 2026
