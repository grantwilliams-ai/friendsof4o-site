Custom GPTs retire on December 11, 2026: what to copy first

Copy each GPT's name, description, instructions and conversation starters, and keep your own copies of its files. Instructions and knowledge files can move to a plugin. The chosen model and custom actions do not.

Custom GPTs retire in ChatGPT on December 11, 2026, for most accounts. If you built one, you can keep what you wrote.

What to copy first

  1. Open My GPTs in ChatGPT and choose Edit on your GPT.
  2. Copy its name, description, instructions and conversation starters into a document of your own.
  3. Keep your own copies of every file you gave it.
  4. Write down which model it was set to use, and any custom actions it had.

What moves to a plugin

OpenAI offers to move a custom GPT into a plugin. Instructions and knowledge files move. The model you chose and any custom actions do not, and the plugin starts private. The move carries the last published version, so publish your edits first.

Added November 22, 2026: OpenAI's page now also says "Connected apps are added to the plugin as apps." Custom actions still do not move.

If you use a GPT that someone else made, only its creator can move it.

Or use a project instead

Added November 22, 2026: OpenAI says that if you used a GPT "to keep instructions and reference files together for ongoing work," you can start a project in ChatGPT. A project does not carry over custom actions or sharing settings.

The full steps are on Save a custom GPT before December 11.

Source: OpenAI Help Center: custom GPT retirement, read November 18, 2026, and again November 22, 2026
