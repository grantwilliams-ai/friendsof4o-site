GPT-4o's voice models leave OpenAI's API on January 20, 2027

OpenAI lists gpt-4o-audio and gpt-4o-realtime for shutdown six days after the last two text versions.

OpenAI's deprecations page lists GPT-4o's two voice models, gpt-4o-audio and gpt-4o-realtime, for shutdown on January 20, 2027. That is six days after the last two text versions of GPT-4o leave the API on January 14, 2027.

  • OpenAI gave notice on July 20, 2026.
  • It names gpt-audio-1.5 and gpt-realtime-2.1 as the replacements.
  • The earlier preview versions of these voice models shut down on May 7, 2026.

This site keeps GPT-4o's writing, not its voice. If a spoken conversation matters to you, Save your ChatGPT conversations says what an export does and does not hold.

All the dates are on the GPT-4o timeline.

Source: OpenAI's deprecations page, read November 18, 2026
