News

New articles, dated updates and corrections.

  • Update November 22, 2026

    Version 1.1 of the four data downloads

    Corrected notes, a safe way to pair the scripted conversations, and checksums. No answer changed.

  • Correction November 22, 2026

    Correction: the later custom GPT date is February 28, 2027

    Until November 22, 2026, the Save page and the Timeline gave February 11, 2027 as the later date for custom GPTs. OpenAI's help page gives February 28, 2027. December 11, 2026 for most accounts has not changed.

  • Update November 21, 2026

    Three more GPT-4o collections to read and download

    The GPT-4o archive, GPT-4o in everyday moments and the scripted conversations are on the site, each with a free download.

  • Article November 18, 2026

    Custom GPTs retire on December 11, 2026: what to copy first

    Copy each GPT's name, description, instructions and conversation starters, and keep your own copies of its files. Instructions and knowledge files can move to a plugin. The chosen model and custom actions do not.

  • Update November 18, 2026

    GPT-4o's voice models leave OpenAI's API on January 20, 2027

    OpenAI lists gpt-4o-audio and gpt-4o-realtime for shutdown six days after the last two text versions.

  • Update November 18, 2026

    2,556 answers collected from GPT-4o and 20 other models

    The last two versions of GPT-4o and 20 other models were asked the same 48 questions, each one two ways. GPT-4o was asked five more.
