Three more GPT-4o collections to read and download

The GPT-4o archive, GPT-4o in everyday moments and the scripted conversations are on the site, each with a free download.

Three collections were added to the site on November 21, 2026. Each can be read on the page and downloaded whole.

  • The GPT-4o archive: 2,157 answers from ChatGPT's own GPT-4o, saved from December 2025 to February 2026.
  • GPT-4o in everyday moments: 1,280 written replies to everyday messages and short conversations. There is no audio.
  • Scripted conversations with the 2024 versions of GPT-4o: 8,286 answers, most of them given inside test conversations that this project scripted.

The comparison of GPT-4o with other models has its own download too. All four are on GPT-4o data sets.

Also new since November 19: An early experiment, one long ChatGPT conversation from August 10, 2025.
