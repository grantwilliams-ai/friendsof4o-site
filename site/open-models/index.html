Downloadable 4o-style models

Downloadable models whose makers say they sound like GPT-4o

Models that anyone can download and run, whose makers say they were trained to sound like GPT-4o.

These are different models. None of them is GPT-4o. An open model is one whose files are public: you run it on your own computer, or on one you rent.

Every claim about style on this page is the maker's own. None of these models has a public evaluation against GPT-4o.

We asked each of them the same questions as GPT-4o, on our own computer. There is no result yet on how close any of them sounds.

  1. Helcyon GPT-4o (Gemma 4 12B)

    By XeyonAI

    The maker says it was fine-tuned to recreate GPT-4o's warmth, humor and conversational style, on GPT-4o data that it does not name.

    Built on
    Gemma 4 12B, according to the maker's page
    License

    Download size
    8.55 GB or 9.79 GB, by quality. A separate 175 MB file lets it read images.

    By trentmkelly (the add-on) and Meta (the base model)
