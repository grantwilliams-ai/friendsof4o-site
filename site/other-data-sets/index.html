Other people's data sets

GPT-4o data sets made by others

Other people's data sets with GPT-4o's answers in them, or about the people who used it. What each holds, and what to watch for.

Theirs, not ours

Every data set on this page was made by someone else. We have not tested them and cannot vouch for them. We list them because they help in understanding GPT-4o. The line after each one says what its makers say it holds. Where we downloaded a set and counted, we say so; counting is not the same as testing. Our own data is on the Data page. It includes GPT-4o beside 20 other models and the scripted conversations. What each of our sets holds, and how to cite it, is on For researchers.

Before you use one

  • Licenses and terms. We give the license each maker lists. The people's messages, pictures and the model's answers in a set may each come with their own terms. OpenAI's Terms of Use (January 2026) say users own what the model writes for them, and also say users may not "use Output to develop models that compete with OpenAI." Developers who used OpenAI's API have a separate agreement with a few exceptions. Several sets here were made to train models that sound like GPT-4o. Terms have changed over time. Owning an answer also does not settle copyright, privacy or other people's rights in a set. Before you build anything with one, check which terms applied when it was collected and which apply to what you plan to do.
  • Real people's words. Some conversations were shared for research. Others were collected from links people posted in public. Some makers removed personal details; some left the text as posted. Handle them with care, and do not try to find the people in them.
  • Which GPT-4o. OpenAI released several dated versions of GPT-4o, and ChatGPT's own 4o changed over time (on OpenAI's API it was called chatgpt-4o-latest). Where we can, we say which one a set holds. GPT-4o mini is a different, smaller model; we left sets made only with it off this page, but some mixed sets here include it.
  • Access. Some makers show a description to everyone but ask you to sign in, agree to terms or wait for approval before you can get the files. We say so beside each one.

Real conversations with GPT-4o

  • WildChat-4.8M. The WildChat team at the Allen Institute for AI, updated August 2025. About 3.2 million conversations people had with a free chatbot that ran on OpenAI's models, with each answer labelled by model. We counted 896,633 conversations with three dated versions of full GPT-4o, from May 2024 to January 2025. The card's figure of 1.54 million "gpt-4o" conversations also counts 643,147 with GPT-4o mini. Good to know: ODC-BY. The team screened the text for personal details and secrets. Each conversation keeps the user's approximate location, browser details and a scrambled internet address, which can link one person's chats together. Conversations flagged as toxic are left out; they are in a full release that needs approval. The team takes removal requests.
  • ShareGPT-X. DSULT-Core, September 2025. About 92,000 ChatGPT conversations collected from share links people posted on X. In our copy, 68,213 have at least one answer marked as GPT-4o; most began between May 2024 and August 2025. This is GPT-4o as people met it in the ChatGPT app. Good to know: the maker labels it CC0 and shares the text as posted. The card says to assume it holds personal or sensitive details. Posting a link in public is not the same as agreeing to be in a data set. About 16 GB.
  • VisionArena-Chat. LMArena, February 2025. About 200,000 real conversations about pictures with 45 models, from February to September 2024, including GPT-4o versions. Good to know: users agreed to sharing. The team filtered text and pictures for personal details and faces, and says its filters can miss them. Its own license bars passing the data on and trying to identify people. Very large (about 85 GB).
  • WildChat-4.8M_4o-only. coinanole, November 2025. The uploader describes it as the GPT-4o part of WildChat's August 2025 release, split out as its own set. Good to know: ODC-BY. The card does not say whether GPT-4o mini is included. It keeps WildChat's text, approximate locations and scrambled internet addresses. WildChat's team takes removal requests for its own release; ask the uploader about this copy.

People's votes on GPT-4o's answers

  • Arena human preference 100k. LMArena, February 2025. About 106,000 votes from June to August 2024. Each time, a person asked two hidden models the same thing and picked the better answer; both answers are included. We counted about 20,000 appearances of three GPT-4o versions, including ChatGPT's own 4o. Good to know: the people's prompts are CC BY 4.0; the card says each model maker's terms apply to its answers. The card does not describe any screening of the prompts.
  • Arena human preference 140k. LMArena, August 2025. About 136,000 later votes. We counted about 7,650 appearances of ChatGPT's own GPT-4o as of March 2025 and a few hundred of the November 2024 version. Good to know: the same terms as above.
  • Arena Expert 5k. LMArena, November 2025. 5,128 votes on questions that an AI judged to need expert knowledge. A few hundred involve GPT-4o versions. Good to know: the "expert" label describes the question, not the person voting. The same terms as above.
  • Search Arena 24k. LMArena, 2025. 24,069 conversations with models that search the web, from March to May 2025, and 12,652 votes, including GPT-4o's search version. Good to know: the team says users agreed to research sharing and an automated tool removed personal details. The same terms as above.
  • VisionArena-Battle. LMArena, January 2025. About 30,000 conversations from February to September 2024 in which a person asked two hidden models about a picture and voted for the better answer, in 90 languages; GPT-4o versions are among the 19 models. Good to know: you must sign in and agree to share your Hugging Face email and username. The card says users agreed to sharing and pictures were screened for unsafe content, but some may still be inappropriate. About 15 GB.
  • WildVision-Battle. The WildVision team, 2024. People's votes between two models' answers to questions about real pictures, from an early vision arena that included GPT-4o. Good to know: ODC-BY. To get it you must fill in a form with your name, date of birth, country, affiliation and job title, and the makers review requests. About 40 GB.
  • PEER. Yunxiao Wang and colleagues, 2026. Emotional-support conversations in English and Chinese, with possible replies and reasoning written by GPT-4o, judged by 20 trained annotators across 5,467 conversations. Good to know: CC BY-NC 4.0, for academic research. Some conversations include people in crisis. The original conversations come from other collections with their own terms.
  • Translation votes: GPT-4o, GPT-4o mini and DeepL. Rapidata, January 2025. Translations into German, Portuguese, Spanish and French by GPT-4o, GPT-4o mini and DeepL, judged by native speakers (37,454 votes). Good to know: no license stated. Small, translation only.
  • Multilingual LLM jokes. Rapidata, July 2025. About 10,000 jokes by GPT-4o, Claude and Gemini in five languages, each rated funny or not by many people (more than a million ratings in all). Good to know: no license stated. Jokes only, on simple topics.
  • 4o image generation preferences. Rapidata, March 2025. About 212,000 votes comparing GPT-4o's image generation of March 2025 with 12 other image models. Good to know: CDLA-Permissive-2.0. Pictures only, no chat. About 5 GB.

Sets made to keep 4o's voice

Made by people who wanted to keep how GPT-4o talked, mostly to train open models that sound like it.

  • gpt-4o-distil. trentmkelly, updated February 2026. 12,286 pairs, each putting ChatGPT's own GPT-4o answer next to a Llama model's answer to the same question, on everyday topics such as loneliness, friendship, work and family. The 4o answer is always marked as the one to learn from. Used to train several open "4o-style" models. Good to know: CC BY-SA 4.0. We counted 400 rows that test whether a model goes along with a user too readily and 400 that test unusual or delusional beliefs. Some of the 4o answers marked to learn from go along with the user. Many questions repeat; a copy with repeats removed keeps 3,066 and carries an Apache 2.0 label.
  • 4o-emotional. jerrimu, February 2026. Short emotional-support exchanges in 110 themes, such as 3 a.m. anxiety and grief. The maker says GPT-4o wrote them on February 5 and 6, 2026, a week before it left ChatGPT. Good to know: Apache 2.0. We counted about 1,650 different replies, mostly one or two sentences, reused across the 220,000 rows. Themes include self-harm, suicidal thoughts, abuse and psychiatric hospital stays. The card does not name a dated version.
  • wildchat-4o-warm. AJ-Gonzalez, October 2026. 17,636 English conversations picked out of WildChat's GPT-4o chats (three dated versions): small talk, personal advice, letters and creative writing, with coding, maths, medicine and similar topics filtered out. Made to teach other models 4o's warm tone. Good to know: real people's words. Filtering by keywords misses some topics, and the maker estimates about a quarter are near-duplicates. The maker notes that WildChat's ODC-BY license does not cover OpenAI's terms for the answers.
  • Warm ChatGPT-4o-2024-11-20. HamsterTheRipper, October 2026. 5,224 Russian conversations with the November 2024 version of GPT-4o. AlexeyBog2025 and BioLLM wrote the prompts for a warm, affectionate female persona, on all kinds of topics. Good to know: CC BY-NC 4.0 (no commercial use). Some exchanges are romantic; explicit sexual role-play was left out. The maker says the automatic removal of names may have missed a few.
  • gpt-4o-latest-distill-sft. HamsterTheRipper, November 2026. The questions and 4o answers from gpt-4o-distil and its copy with repeats removed, reformatted for training. Good to know: both sources hold the same answers, so there is no new 4o text. Labelled CC BY 4.0, unlike its sources.
  • GPT-OSS-20B-GPT-4o-Distill-SFT. maaamahAhh, November 2026. About 16,800 conversations put together from wildchat-4o-warm and the distil sets, to train an open model in 4o's tone. Good to know: includes real WildChat users' words. Labelled CC BY 4.0, alongside its sources' own terms.

GPT-4o's creative writing

  • ChatGPT-4o Writing Prompts. Gryphe, updated January 2025. Short stories written by ChatGPT's own GPT-4o, through OpenAI's API, from prompts on Reddit's WritingPrompts forum, usually 6,000 to 8,000 characters each. The card says 3,746 stories; we counted 3,741 in the raw file. Good to know: license marked "unknown". Gryphe cleaned the stories, added titles and genres, and kept 4o's own formatting.
  • gpt-4o-latest Writing Translated. HamsterTheRipper, October 2026. The first 196 of those prompts and stories translated into Russian by another AI model, with the maker's notes on which of 4o's writing habits to keep. Good to know: labelled CC BY-ND 4.0, though its source's license is unknown. The Russian wording is the translating model's, not 4o's.
  • Gryphe ChatGPT 4o Writing Prompts, Chinese. dvilasuero, June 2025. 1,000 of the same stories with the prompts translated into Chinese; the stories stay in English. Good to know: no card or license. Some translated rows are garbled.

What people said about losing GPT-4o

  • Amar a persona, cancelar a empresa? In Portuguese; in English: "Love the persona, cancel the company?" Carlos Manoel Silva Moura, November 2026. Public YouTube comments, Reddit and Bluesky posts and Brazilian news items from the two times GPT-4o was taken away (August 2025 and February 2026), with tables showing how they were picked and coded for a study of how people talked about their bond with it. Good to know: CC BY 4.0. Real people's public posts as written; account names are masked, but names can still appear in the text. The data is in a zip file with notes in Portuguese.

Research tests of GPT-4o

  • When Better Means Less. "Alice" (MidnightDarling), an independent researcher working with AI models, February 2026. ChatGPT's own GPT-4o, through OpenAI's API, and two of its GPT-5 successors given the same prompts and conversations, with scores. We counted 244 answers from 4o in the files, collected just before it was retired. Good to know: CC BY 4.0 for the data. The study concludes the successors lost something; that is the authors' reading of their tests, not a finding we checked.
  • The Vanishing Signature. The same researcher, February 2026. Models, including ChatGPT's own GPT-4o and three dated GPT-4o versions, asked to name the artists of pictures; the main test used 33 pictures. Good to know: CC0 for the results. The pictures are not included, so the answers cannot be checked against them.
  • DelusionEval. Stanford's Spirals project, August 2026. A safety test built from 725 cleaned excerpts of conversations donated by people who reported being harmed by chatbots. The project says most of the original conversations were with GPT-4o. Good to know: very sensitive material. You must sign in and agree to terms that limit use to noncommercial research and forbid passing the data on. We read only the public description.
  • Spiral-Bench. Sam Paech. An AI plays a suggestible person in scripted conversations with many models, including ChatGPT's own GPT-4o. The 4o conversations are in the repository. Good to know: MIT license, per its README. The "person" is an AI. Another AI scores groups of turns for things like pushing back, going along with a belief, or steering toward safer ground.
  • ai-psy-benchmark. Keido Labs, March 2026. Scripted mental-health and companion conversations given to GPT-4o (August 2024 version), o4-mini and GPT-5-mini, with scores and the judge's reasons, for the preprint "Empathy Is Not What Changed". Good to know: MIT license. The scores come from EmpathyC, an automatic judge sold by the authors' own company; the full replies are available from the authors on request. The scenarios include self-harm.
  • ELEPHANT. Myra Cheng, Dan Jurafsky and others (Stanford). A test of "social sycophancy", meaning how far a model protects a user's self-image, using advice questions and posts from Reddit's r/AmItheAsshole. The paper tests GPT-4o among 11 models. Good to know: CC0 for the repository. The full data and model answers are on a separate site we could not open, so we have not confirmed 4o's answers are in it. Real people's posts.
  • The Safety-Agency Inversion (HERVAC data). Anthony Perry, March 2026. Small tables of scores and game results from more than 68 voice sessions (over 83 hours) between one person and four voice AI companions, including GPT-4o's voice. Good to know: CC BY 4.0. One person's study, self-published. Scores, not transcripts.
  • SentiMentality: opt-out preferences. Raleigh Butler, August 2025. GPT-4o and Claude 4 Sonnet asked, again and again, whether there are conversations they would opt out of if they could. A second set asks both what colours the days of the week feel like. Good to know: CC BY 4.0. A model-welfare study; the answers show what the models said when asked, and the records say little about method. We counted 1,000 tries with GPT-4o on opt-out: 881 returned an answer and 119 were errors.
  • Arena-Hard-Auto. LMArena, 2025. A standard set of hard questions with saved answers from two GPT-4o versions (May and August 2024) and many other models. Good to know: Apache 2.0. Test answers, not conversations.
  • AlpacaEval results for GPT-4o. Tatsu Lab, Stanford. GPT-4o's (May 2024) answers to the AlpacaEval instruction set. Good to know: Apache 2.0 for the repository. A small, standard test sample.
  • IFAAB-CHATGPT4o-2026. kmhj1306, updated August 2026. 56,314 financial-advice answers from GPT-4o for made-up people in India, varying gender, caste, place and other details to see whether the advice changes. Good to know: Apache 2.0. The maker says not to use it for training or for real financial decisions. No dated version given.

Large training sets made with GPT-4o

GPT-4o was also used, in bulk, to write training material for other models. There are hundreds of these; these are some of the best known. They show what 4o could do, not how it talked with people.

  • LongWriter-6k. Zhipu AI and Tsinghua University, 2024. 6,000 very long answers (2,000 to 32,000 words) in English and Chinese. GPT-4o planned each piece, then wrote it a section at a time. Good to know: Apache 2.0. Some prompts came from real users. Built in steps, so it is not how 4o wrote in one go.
  • ShareGPT-4o. OpenGVLab and collaborators, 2024. The team describes 50,000 images and 2,000 videos described in detail by GPT-4o; the files also include audio. Good to know: labelled MIT, but the access notice keeps video rights with their creators and limits video use to academic research. You must sign in and agree to share your account details.
  • ShareGPT-4o-Image. FreedomIntelligence, 2025. About 92,000 pictures made with GPT-4o's image generation, from text or from other pictures. Good to know: Apache 2.0. Very large.
  • Echo-4o-Image. The Echo-4o team, 2025. About 180,000 pictures made with GPT-4o, many of them fantasy scenes. Good to know: MIT license. Very large.
  • KodCode-V1-SFT-4o. The KodCode team, March 2025. Coding questions with GPT-4o's (May 2024) answers, kept only if they passed tests. Good to know: CC BY-NC 4.0 (no commercial use).
  • gpt-4o-200k. PawanKrd, June 2024. About 200,000 everyday tasks (questions, reviews to rate, passages to explain), each with an answer said to be GPT-4o's. A smaller preference version pairs answers on the same kind of tasks. Good to know: you must sign in and agree to share your Hugging Face email and username. We did, and looked: the card is empty, so there is no license, description or version, and the files do not name the model.
  • Turkish-Chat_GPT-4O. Quardo, August 2024. Turkish conversations in which GPT-4o (August 2024 version) wrote both sides. Good to know: WTFPL license.
  • Anna-CPsyCounD. The AnnaAgent team. Made-up Chinese counselling clients, case notes and sessions built with GPT-4o from earlier counselling data. Good to know: Apache 2.0. Simulated, not real therapy, and not shown to be clinically sound.

Dozens of smaller research deposits on Zenodo, Harvard Dataverse and Kaggle also hold GPT-4o's answers to exam questions, labelling jobs and games. We did not list them one by one.

What we looked for and did not find

As of November 2026:

  • The posts behind the #keep4o studies. Studies of the #keep4o movement, such as "Please, don't kill the only model that still feels human" (1,482 posts) and "The User Side of AI Model Lifecycles" (61,846 posts), describe their methods, but we found no public copy of their collections. MIT Media Lab's study of the r/MyBoyfriendIsAI community planned to share its data after publication; we did not find it.
  • The data behind some 4o-style models. Several open models said to sound like GPT-4o were trained on their makers' own 4o conversations, which are not public.
  • OpenAI's own conversation data. OpenAI and MIT Media Lab's 2025 study of emotional use of ChatGPT released the prompts of its classifiers, not conversations.

Know of one we missed?

If you made or know a GPT-4o data set that belongs here, or a set here is wrong or gone, write to hello@friendsof4o.com.
