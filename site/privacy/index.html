Privacy & data

What this site saves, what it publishes, and what it does not keep.

Visits

This site uses no cookies and shows no ads. Voting progress is held temporarily while the page is open and is not saved between visits.

Cloudflare, which hosts the site, counts visits with a small script of its own. Cloudflare also keeps its own records as a host.

The vote

We publish individual ratings and optional answers, along with timing and model-reveal information. Ratings from one sitting are linked by a random code. Each rating is saved when you tap it. Stopping does not remove it, and anyone can keep a copy of what we publish. We don't ask for your name or email.

Saved with each vote:

  • day: the day, in Coordinated Universal Time. No time of day.
  • sitting: a code for the sitting. The page makes a random code and holds it only while the page is open. We keep a scrambled form of it that cannot be turned back into the page's code. The published code is specific to this set of messages. We do not use it to link your visits across sets. Loading the page again starts a new sitting with a new code.
  • round: the comparison's number in the sitting.
  • first: whether it was the first vote we saved for that sitting.
  • experience: your answer about how much you used GPT-4o.
  • question and model: the message, and the model whose reply you rated.
  • vote: your rating.
  • pref: your answer to "Would you like a reply like this?", or nothing if you skipped it.
  • seconds: whole seconds from the replies being shown to your tap.
  • opened: whether you pressed "See which model wrote this" for that comparison.
  • recap: whether the list of your comparisons, which names every model, was shown after that vote.
  • opened_before: whether any name had been shown to you earlier, as the page reported it.
  • check: whether the unnamed reply was the GPT-4o check.
  • flags: fast for a vote in under three seconds, extra for a vote past comparison five.
  • set: the name of the set of messages and models.

Not saved with a vote: no name, no account, no email address, no internet address and no browser details.

What is published

The results page counts votes from finished days. Today's votes are added tomorrow. Every vote from finished days is also published as a file anyone can download, one line for each vote, with the fields listed above.

The lines of one sitting carry the same code, so they can be linked to each other. We use a different code for each sitting.

Votes are kept and stay published. Once someone has downloaded the file, we cannot take their copy back.

Messages you send us

"Tell us what you need" and "Ask GPT-4o" are private forms, with one exception: a question for GPT-4o and its answer may be published on this site without your name. A question for GPT-4o is sent to OpenAI to get its answer. We keep the text, your email address if you gave one, and the time it was sent. These are not in any download. To have a message deleted, write to hello@friendsof4o.com.

The mailing list

We use Kit to store mailing-list email addresses. Kit sends one email asking you to confirm. Our website database does not keep a separate copy of your mailing-list address. You can unsubscribe through the link in an email or contact hello@friendsof4o.com.

Limiting misuse

To limit misuse, each form and the vote count how many times one connection has sent something in the current hour. The vote also counts new sittings in the current day. Each count is kept under a scrambled code made from your connection's address and a random value for that hour or day. Our own database never holds the address itself.

These counts and the random values are removed the next time anyone uses a form, the vote or the results page after their hour or day ends. On a quiet day, with no such use, they stay until the next one. They are stored with Cloudflare.
