Can I still use GPT-4o?

Not in ChatGPT. GPT-4o left ChatGPT on February 13, 2026. Two versions of it still run on OpenAI's API, for developers, until January 14, 2027, and on Microsoft Azure, for its customers, until April 14, 2027.

Where GPT-4o still runs

Sources: OpenAI: Retiring GPT-4o and older models; OpenAI's deprecations page, read November 22, 2026; Microsoft's retirement page, read November 19, 2026

Is GPT-4o coming back?

Not that anyone has announced. It came back once: GPT-5 replaced it in ChatGPT on August 7, 2025, and it returned for paid users on August 12. OpenAI's retirement notice says nothing about a second return.

Is GPT-4o coming back?

Sources: ChatGPT release notes; OpenAI: Retiring GPT-4o and older models, read November 22, 2026

When does GPT-4o leave the API?

The August 2024 and November 2024 versions shut down on OpenAI's API on January 14, 2027. GPT-4o's voice models shut down on January 20, 2027. OpenAI names GPT-6 Luna as the replacement for the text versions.

The GPT-4o timeline

Source: OpenAI's deprecations page, read November 22, 2026

Do custom GPTs survive?

Custom GPTs retire in ChatGPT on December 11, 2026, for most accounts. OpenAI offers to move one into a plugin, which keeps its instructions and knowledge files but not the model you chose. Your existing conversations with it remain.

Save a custom GPT before December 11

Source: OpenAI Help Center: custom GPT retirement, read November 22, 2026

How do I export my ChatGPT chats?

In ChatGPT on the web, open Settings, then Data controls, then Export data. The export can take up to 7 days to arrive, and its download link expires 24 hours after you receive it. Copy your saved memories and custom instructions by hand as well.

Save your ChatGPT conversations

Source: OpenAI Help Center: how to export your ChatGPT history and data, read November 19, 2026

Which model is most like GPT-4o?

There is no measured answer yet, and no other model is the same. Every model on the Models page was asked the same questions as GPT-4o, so you can read its answers beside GPT-4o's and choose for yourself.

Can I run something like GPT-4o at home?

Some open models are said by their makers to be trained to sound like GPT-4o. They are different models, and none of them is GPT-4o. Their files are public, so you can run them on your own computer or on one you rent.

Open models trained to sound like GPT-4o

What is the difference between ChatGPT's GPT-4o and the API versions?

ChatGPT's own GPT-4o was the version inside the ChatGPT app. The API versions are fixed copies for developers, dated August 2024 and November 2024. ChatGPT's own version left the API on February 17, 2026, and the dated copies are what remain.

Where can I find support?

If you are grieving GPT-4o or having a hard time, the Support page lists free lines you can call or text in many countries. You do not have to be in crisis to use them. If you are in immediate danger, call your local emergency number.
