Run a 4o-style model on your own computer

One free model, one free app, and the steps to start a chat on your own computer. It is not GPT-4o, and we say what it is.

  3. Step 2: Download the model
  4. Step 3: Load it and start a chat

What this is, and what it is not

What it is. Nova-Qwen-14B is a model anyone can download. Its maker says it was fine-tuned on 3,066 GPT-4o conversations. With a free app called LM Studio, it runs on your own computer. It costs nothing, and your chats stay with you.

What it is not. It is not GPT-4o. It will not know your old chats. We asked it the same questions as GPT-4o, and there is no result yet on how close it sounds. You can read its answers beside GPT-4o's and judge for yourself.

Sources: Downloadable models said to sound like GPT-4o (the maker's claim, as this site records it)

What you need

  • A Mac with an Apple chip (M1, M2, M3 or M4) and macOS 14 or newer. Macs with an Intel chip cannot run LM Studio.
  • Or a Windows PC. On most PCs the processor must support AVX2. PCs with a Snapdragon X Elite chip are supported too. LM Studio recommends a graphics card with at least 4 GB of its own memory.
  • 16 GB of memory (RAM). LM Studio recommends at least that much. The model file has to fit in memory while it runs, so an 8 GB computer is too small for this one.
  • About 9 GB of free disk space for the model file, plus room for the app.
  • An internet connection to download the app and the model. After that, chatting works offline.

Sources: LM Studio: System Requirements; LM Studio: Offline Operation; Hugging Face: Lighstromo/Nova-Qwen-14B-GGUF, all read November 22, 2026

Step 1: Install LM Studio

  1. Go to LM Studio's download page.
  2. Download the installer for your computer (macOS or Windows).
  3. Open it and install it the way you would any other program.

Running models on your own computer is LM Studio's free plan. It also sells paid plans for models it runs online. You do not need them for this.

Sources: LM Studio: Get started; LM Studio: Download; LM Studio: Pricing, all read November 22, 2026

Step 2: Download the model

  1. Open LM Studio and go to the Discover tab. The shortcut is ⌘ + 2 on a Mac, or Ctrl + 2 on Windows.
  2. In the search bar, type Lighstromo/Nova-Qwen-14B-GGUF. You can also paste its address: https://huggingface.co/Lighstromo/Nova-Qwen-14B-GGUF.
  3. You will see two choices. Pick Q4_K_M, the 8.99 GB file. The other, Q6_K, is 12.1 GB and needs more memory.
  4. Download it. This can take a while on a slow connection.

The two choices are the same model, compressed by different amounts. A smaller file needs less memory and gives up a little quality. LM Studio's advice is to choose a 4-bit option or higher if your computer can run it; Q4_K_M is the 4-bit one. We ran the larger Q6_K file for our own test. What "compressed" means.

Sources: LM Studio: Download an LLM; Hugging Face: Lighstromo/Nova-Qwen-14B-GGUF, read November 22, 2026

Step 3: Load it and start a chat

  1. Go to the Chat tab.
  2. Open the model loader. The shortcut is ⌘ + L on a Mac, or Ctrl + L on Windows.
  3. Choose Nova-Qwen-14B from the models you downloaded. LM Studio now puts it into your computer's memory.
  4. Type a message and send it.

To start a new chat, click +, or press ⌘ + N on a Mac, Ctrl + N on Windows.

The answer appears a few words at a time. How fast depends on your computer.

Sources: LM Studio: Get started; LM Studio: Manage chats, read November 22, 2026

Step 4: Give it the February instruction

A system prompt is a short instruction the model reads before every message. It changes how a model answers.

  • Which words. Copy the February instruction from the Style page. It is the paragraph this project used to ask for GPT-4o's warmth and directness. In our one test it brought GPT-4o's own opening closer to how ChatGPT's GPT-4o opened.
  • Why it matters for this model. If you give Nova-Qwen-14B no instruction, its own setup adds Qwen's standard opening instruction instead. Its maker's page suggests one of its own: "You are Nova, a warm, witty, and thoughtful AI assistant."
  • Where it goes. LM Studio keeps system prompts in Presets, in the settings sidebar. A Preset saves your system prompt so every new chat starts with it. If you see only the chat and no settings, LM Studio is in User mode, which shows only the chat. Turn on Developer mode in Settings, then Developer, to see every setting.

Sources: LM Studio: Config Presets; LM Studio: User or Developer; Hugging Face: Lighstromo/Nova-Qwen-14B-GGUF, read November 22, 2026; How this was made

The license

Nova-Qwen-14B is released under the Apache 2.0 license, which lets anyone use, copy and share it for free, as long as a copy of the license goes with it.

Sources: Hugging Face: Lighstromo/Nova-Qwen-14B-GGUF; Apache License 2.0, read November 22, 2026

Your privacy

LM Studio's documentation says "nothing you enter into LM Studio when chatting with LLMs leaves your device", and it saves your chats as files on your own computer.

Sources: LM Studio: Offline Operation; LM Studio: Manage chats, read November 22, 2026

Same steps, different file

The other models on the open models page work the same way: search for the name in Step 2 and pick a file that fits your memory. What differs:

  • Helcyon-4o v4.0 (Mistral Nemo 12B). Files of 8.73 GB or 10.06 GB. Apache 2.0. Its maker's page advertises "Zero Guardrails".
  • Helcyon-GPT-4o v4.5 (Mistral Nemo 12B). 10.06 GB for the version we checked. Apache 2.0. Its maker's page says it has "no baked-in refusals".
  • Helcyon GPT-4o (Gemma 4 12B). 8.55 GB or 9.79 GB. Its maker states no license. Asked with no instruction, it often answered something other than the question in our test.
  • Aura-4o-Rebirth (Gemma 4 31B). Apache 2.0. The version we ran is 21.85 GB, too large for a 16 GB computer.
  • GPT-4o distillation on Llama 3.1 8B and GPT-4o distillation on Llama 3.3 70B. These are add-ons, not complete files: each is applied to a separate model from Meta, under Meta's Llama license. The steps on this page do not cover add-ons, and most people cannot run the 70B one.

Sources: Downloadable models said to sound like GPT-4o; Hugging Face: XeyonAI/MN-Helcyon-4o-12b-v4.0-GGUF; Hugging Face: XeyonAI/MN-Helcyon-GPT-4o-12b-v4.5-GGUF, read November 22, 2026

If it does not work

These are the limits LM Studio's own documentation names:

  • Your Mac has an Intel chip. LM Studio does not support Intel Macs.
  • Your Mac runs a macOS older than 14. LM Studio needs macOS 14 or newer.
  • Your Mac has 8 GB of memory. LM Studio says to stick to smaller models on 8 GB Macs. This model is too large for one.
  • Your Windows PC's processor lacks AVX2. LM Studio needs it on PCs with an x64 processor, which is most of them.
  • Search or download does nothing. Finding and downloading models needs the internet. Chatting with a model you already have does not.

Sources: LM Studio: System Requirements; LM Studio: Offline Operation, read November 22, 2026
