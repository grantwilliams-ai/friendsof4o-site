Save

Save (export) your ChatGPT conversations

An export keeps the words. It cannot keep the model. Here is how to save what is yours, in two parts: your chats, and your custom GPTs.

On this page

  1. Part 1: Save your chats
  2. Part 2: Save a custom GPT
  3. What saving does not do

Dates to know

December 11, 2026. Custom GPTs retire in ChatGPT for most accounts on that day. If you built one, start with part 2: save a custom GPT.

December 11, 2026 has passed. Custom GPTs retired in ChatGPT for most accounts on that day. FedRAMP workspaces, and Enterprise workspaces with an approved deferral, have until February 28, 2027. If yours is one of them, start with part 2: save a custom GPT.

An account export takes time. It can take up to 7 days to arrive, and its download link expires 24 hours after you receive it.

Sources: OpenAI Help Center: custom GPT retirement, read November 18, 2026, dates read again November 22, 2026; OpenAI Help Center: how to export your ChatGPT history and data, read November 19, 2026

Part 1: Save your chats

GPT-4o left ChatGPT on February 13, 2026. What you can still keep is what it wrote with you, and what you wrote to shape it.

Request your account export

  1. Sign in to ChatGPT on the web and open your profile menu.
  2. Choose Settings, then Data controls.
  3. Under Export data, choose Export, then Confirm export.
  4. Watch your email or text messages. The export can take up to 7 days to arrive.
  5. Download it as soon as it arrives. The download link expires 24 hours after you receive it.

On a phone, the same steps are under Settings, Data controls, Export data. If the download link will not open on your phone, try the same link on a computer while signed in.

This works for Free, Go, Plus, Pro and eligible Edu accounts. It does not work for Business, Enterprise or ChatGPT for Healthcare workspaces. Ask the workspace owner.

If you cannot sign in, request your data through OpenAI's Privacy Portal.

Source: OpenAI Help Center: how to export your ChatGPT history and data, read November 19, 2026

Check that it worked

  1. Find the download on a computer. It is a ZIP file. Unzip it into a folder.
  2. In that folder, open index.html in a web browser. It is OpenAI's export viewer.
  3. In the viewer, select Choose folder, then pick the unzipped folder.
  4. Look for a conversation you remember, an old one and a recent one.
  5. Keep the whole ZIP file in two places, such as your computer and a drive of your own.

If the export seems incomplete, contact OpenAI Support and say what is missing.

Your conversations are stored in JSON format. Other data may be in CSV or other formats. An earlier export may contain chat.html instead of the viewer. That file also opens in a web browser.

Source: OpenAI Help Center: how to export your ChatGPT history and data, read November 19, 2026

An export cannot bring back chats you deleted. OpenAI's export page does not list voice recordings, or a separate folder of images. If a picture or a spoken conversation matters to you, save it yourself.

Source: OpenAI Help Center: how to export your ChatGPT history and data, read November 19, 2026

Copy your memory and your instructions

  1. In ChatGPT, open Settings, then Personalization.
  2. Copy your saved memories into a document of your own.
  3. Copy your custom instructions into the same document. On a phone they are under Customize ChatGPT.

Do this by hand even if you have an export. OpenAI's export page does not say that every saved memory is in the download.

Sources: OpenAI Help Center: memory, OpenAI Help Center: custom instructions, read November 7, 2026; OpenAI Help Center: how to export your ChatGPT history and data, read November 19, 2026

Keep what matters as plain files

Open the export while you can and save the conversations you care about most as ordinary documents. If you used Projects or Canvas, copy what matters from them too.

Part 2: Save a custom GPT

Custom GPTs retire in ChatGPT on December 11, 2026, for most accounts. FedRAMP workspaces, and Enterprise workspaces with an approved deferral, have until February 28, 2027.

Custom GPTs retired in ChatGPT on December 11, 2026, for most accounts. FedRAMP workspaces, and Enterprise workspaces with an approved deferral, have until February 28, 2027. If your workspace still has custom GPTs, the steps below still apply.

An account export takes time. It can take up to 7 days to arrive, and its download link expires 24 hours after you receive it. The steps are in part 1: save your chats.

Copy it yourself first

  1. Open My GPTs in ChatGPT and choose Edit on your GPT.
  2. Copy its name, description, instructions and conversation starters into a document of your own.
  3. Keep your own copies of every file you gave it.
  4. Write down which model it was set to use, and any custom actions it had.

What moves to a plugin, and what does not

OpenAI offers to move a custom GPT into a plugin. The move carries the last published version, so publish any edits you want kept before you start.

Part of your GPT Moves to the plugin?
Instructions Yes. They become a skill in the plugin.
Knowledge files Yes. They become reference files.
Connected apps Yes. OpenAI says: "Connected apps are added to the plugin as apps."
The model you chose No.
Custom actions No. They have to be built again.
Sharing No. The plugin starts private, so other people do not get it automatically.

Your existing conversations with the GPT remain after it retires.

Or use a project instead

OpenAI also says that if you used a GPT "to keep instructions and reference files together for ongoing work," you can start a project in ChatGPT. Add your instructions in the project's settings and upload your files. A project does not carry over custom actions or sharing settings, and your old chats with the GPT cannot be moved into it.

If you use a GPT that someone else made

Only its creator can move it. You can ask them, and you can still save your own conversations with it, as in part 1: save your chats.

Sources: OpenAI Help Center: custom GPT retirement, read November 18, 2026, dates, connected apps and projects read again November 22, 2026; OpenAI Help Center: how to export your ChatGPT history and data, read November 19, 2026

What saving does not do

An export keeps the words. It does not keep the model, and no other model will sound exactly like it. Your history and your instructions are what you can carry to the next one.
