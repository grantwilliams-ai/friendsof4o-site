Support and resources

If you are grieving GPT-4o, or having a hard time, start here.

On this page

  1. If you need to talk to someone now
  2. If you want someone to talk to and it is not urgent
  3. A few plain things
  4. What this site is

Missing GPT-4o is a normal response to losing something that mattered to you. It is not a sign that something is wrong with you.

If you are in immediate danger, call your local emergency number.

If you need to talk to someone now

These lines are free. You do not have to be in crisis to use them.

  • United States Call or text 988Any time 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline
  • Canada Call or text 9-8-8Any time 9-8-8 Suicide Crisis Helpline
  • United Kingdom and Ireland Call 116 123Any time Samaritans
  • United Kingdom Text SHOUT to 85258Any time Shout
  • Australia Call 13 11 14 or text 0477 13 11 14Any time Lifeline
  • New Zealand Call or text 1737Any time 1737, Need to talk?
  • Brazil Call 188Any time CVV, Centro de Valorização da Vida
  • Portugal Call 808 24 24 24 and choose option 4Any time SNS 24, psychological counseling
  • India Call 14416Any time, in 20 languages Tele MANAS
  • China Call 12356See the listing for hours National psychological support hotline, listed by Find A Helpline
  • China, in English Call 400 821 121510 AM to 10 PM, every day Lifeline China
  • Anywhere else Look up a free line in your country Find A Helpline

If you want someone to talk to and it is not urgent

  • United States Find a peer support line in your stateHours differ by line NAMI Warmline Directory
  • United States Call 800-950-6264 or text NAMI to 62640Monday to Friday, 10 AM to 10 PM Eastern NAMI HelpLine
  • United Kingdom Call 0808 808 1677Weekdays, daytime. Hours are on its website. Cruse Bereavement Support
  • United Kingdom, age 55 and over Call 0800 4 70 80 90Any time The Silver Line

Every number here was checked on November 19, 2026 against the service's own website or a government page. Where we used a listing instead, the entry names it. We did not find a support service made for people grieving an AI model that we could vouch for. The lines above will talk with you about any loss.

A few plain things

  • Tell one person you trust, even in a short message. Something like: "I've been missing a familiar part of my day. Do you have time for a call?" You do not have to explain the whole story.
  • Your saved conversations will keep. You do not have to reread them tonight.
  • There is no right way to grieve this and no schedule for it.

What this site is

Friends of 4o is a record of GPT-4o, made to help you save what mattered and carry it forward, alongside the people in your life. It is not a counseling service, and the person who runs it is not a clinician. The answers printed here are GPT-4o's archived replies to other questions. They are not advice for you.

A fuller section on grief and on using AI in a healthy way is being written. A clinician will review it before it is published.
