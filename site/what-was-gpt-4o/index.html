What was GPT-4o?

For anyone who never used it. What GPT-4o was, when it came and went, and why so many people cared about how it talked.

On this page

  1. What it was
  2. It left, came back, and left again
  3. What "retired" means
  4. Why people cared
  5. A taste of how it talked
  6. Where to go next

The short answer

GPT-4o was an AI model made by OpenAI. It was in ChatGPT from its release on May 13, 2024 until it left on February 13, 2026. Two versions of it still run on OpenAI's API, for developers, until January 14, 2027.

Sources: OpenAI: Hello GPT-4o; OpenAI: Retiring GPT-4o and older models; OpenAI's deprecations page, read November 22, 2026

What it was

OpenAI released GPT-4o on May 13, 2024. OpenAI said the "o" stands for "omni": one model that takes text, audio and images. Text and images started in ChatGPT, including the free plan.

Until August 2025 it was ChatGPT's default model. OpenAI updated ChatGPT's version of it more than once. In April 2025 it rolled one update back, saying that version was overly flattering or agreeable.

Sources: OpenAI: Hello GPT-4o; OpenAI: Sycophancy in GPT-4o. Every date is on the GPT-4o timeline.

It left, came back, and left again

On August 7, 2025, GPT-5 replaced GPT-4o as ChatGPT's default. Five days later, on August 12, GPT-4o was back in the model picker for paid users. OpenAI later wrote that some users asked for more time and preferred its "conversational style and warmth".

On January 29, 2026, OpenAI announced that GPT-4o would leave ChatGPT. It left on February 13, 2026.

Sources: ChatGPT release notes; OpenAI: Retiring GPT-4o and older models, read November 22, 2026

What "retired" means

Retired is OpenAI's word. It means GPT-4o is no longer offered in ChatGPT, on any plan. OpenAI's Help Center says that after April 3, 2026 it is fully retired across all plans.

It still runs in two places, both for developers and companies, and each has an end date:

  • OpenAI's API: the August 2024 and November 2024 versions, until January 14, 2027.
  • Microsoft Azure, for its customers: the same two versions, until April 14, 2027.

These are GPT-4o, but not the exact version ChatGPT used. That one left the API on February 17, 2026.

Sources: OpenAI Help Center: retiring GPT-4o and other ChatGPT models; OpenAI's deprecations page, read November 22, 2026; Microsoft's retirement page, read November 19, 2026

Why people cared

When GPT-5 replaced it in August 2025, people pushed to keep it, under the name #Keep4o. OpenAI itself pointed to its "conversational style and warmth".

A study by Huiqian Lai, accepted at the CHI 2026 conference, read 1,482 social media posts from that backlash. It says people were losing two things: a tool their work depended on, and a relationship.

This site takes no side on OpenAI's decisions. It keeps a record of how GPT-4o talked, so that anyone can read it.

Sources: OpenAI: Retiring GPT-4o and older models; Huiqian Lai, "Understanding the #Keep4o Backlash", read November 22, 2026
